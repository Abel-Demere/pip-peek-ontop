# PIP Peek Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** Abugyda  
**Last updated:** November 11, 2026

PIP Peek opens a video from a webpage in a separate player window. It reads video and caption state from pages where it runs to detect videos, show the player control, and synchronize playback and captions.

## Data processed and stored

- Video frames, playback state, and caption text are processed locally in your browser to display and control the detached player. PIP Peek does not send this content to Abugyda.
- Player preferences are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.
- PIP Peek does not include analytics, advertising, account creation, or developer-operated tracking services.
- The extension needs access to HTTP and HTTPS pages to find supported videos and display its in-page video control. It does not use that access to collect or sell browsing history.

## Optional Windows pin support

If you choose to enable optional always-on-top support, PIP Peek downloads the PIP Peek On Top Windows helper from this public GitHub repository. You choose to run the installer. The helper communicates with the extension through Chrome Native Messaging on your computer to pin or unpin the player. It does not upload video, captions, or browsing history to Abugyda.

## Sharing and retention

PIP Peek does not transmit page video, captions, or browsing history to Abugyda or analytics providers. Local preferences remain in Chrome storage until you clear them or remove the extension. GitHub may process requests to download the optional helper under its own privacy terms.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact email shown on the PIP Peek Chrome Web Store listing.
